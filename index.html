PFS FIGHT RULES

THE RULEBOOK.

Website draft of the fight-rule section. Final competition rules should be approved by PFS officials before an event.

Fighters must compete in the designated weight class for their bout. Official weigh-in requirements and any permitted allowance should be stated in event regulations.

Fighters must attend the official weigh-in at the time and location announced by PFS. Failure to meet the required weight may affect bout eligibility.

Required gloves, hand protection and other approved equipment must be used as specified by PFS officials. Equipment must be checked before competition.

Fighters must wear approved competition attire and must not use equipment or clothing that creates an unfair advantage or safety risk.

The number and duration of rounds will be announced for each PFS bout and must be followed by fighters and officials.

Fighters receive the official break between rounds. Only authorized corner personnel may provide assistance during the break.

Only techniques permitted by the specific PFS ruleset and bout classification may be used. Fighters must follow referee instructions.

Any prohibited action is a foul. The referee may issue warnings, deduct points, stop the bout or take another permitted action.

The referee is responsible for fighter safety and control of the bout. Fighters must immediately follow referee commands.

Any knockdown procedure and count will follow the official PFS ruleset applicable to the bout.

Where applicable, officials may use competition rules and medical assessment procedures to determine whether a fighter can safely continue.

A bout may be stopped by the referee, ringside medical professional or other authorized official when continuation is considered unsafe.

When a bout reaches the judges, the official judging criteria and scoring system designated by PFS for that event will determine the result.

A bout may end by a decision or another result recognized by the official PFS ruleset.

If the official scoring system results in an equal score, the bout may be declared a draw where permitted by event rules.

Only authorized corner personnel may be present in the designated corner area and they must follow event officials' instructions.

Fighters may be required to complete pre-bout and post-bout medical checks. Medical officials may recommend that a fighter not compete or continue.

Fighters must compete respectfully and follow official instructions. Serious or repeated misconduct may result in penalties or disqualification under event regulations.

The official result of each bout will be recorded by PFS. Fighter records should be updated only from confirmed official results.
