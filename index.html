OFFICIAL REGULATIONS

Fight Rules

Tap any rule to expand the official PFS regulation.

01+
Fighter EligibilityRegistration and participation requirements.

Official Rule

Every fighter must complete the official PFS registration process and meet all participation requirements before competing.

  • Official registration must be completed before the bout.
  • Required identification and fighter information must be provided.
  • A fighter must be approved by the event officials before entering competition.
02+
Weight ClassesOfficial PFS divisions and limits.

Official Rule

Fighters compete within the weight division assigned to their bout. PFS may publish the exact division limits for each event.

  • The contracted weight must be respected.
  • Any special bout weight must be announced before the event.
  • Final division placement is subject to official weigh-in results.
03+
Weigh-InOfficial pre-bout weight procedure.

Official Rule

All fighters must attend the official PFS weigh-in at the time and location announced by the promotion.

  • Official scales and officials will determine the recorded weight.
  • Fighters must follow the event weigh-in schedule.
  • Any weight discrepancy is handled by the PFS event officials under the event's published procedures.
04+
Medical RequirementsFighter safety and medical approval.

Official Rule

Fighter safety is a priority. Participation requires the medical clearance required by the event and applicable local regulations.

  • Required medical checks must be completed.
  • Medical personnel may recommend that a fighter not compete.
  • Event officials may act on legitimate safety concerns.
05+
Pre-Fight InspectionFinal checks before entering the ring.

Official Rule

Before competition, officials may inspect required equipment, gloves, wraps and other permitted protective equipment.

  • Equipment must meet PFS event requirements.
  • Any issue identified by officials must be corrected before the bout.
  • Final approval rests with the designated event officials.
06+
EquipmentApproved gloves, wraps and protection.

Official Rule

Only equipment approved for the specific PFS event may be used during competition.

  • Gloves must be approved by event officials.
  • Hand wraps must comply with the event's requirements.
  • Additional protective equipment is permitted only when approved by PFS and the applicable rules.
07+
Ring StandardsApproved ring and safety standards.

Official Rule

The competition ring must meet the safety and operational standards established for the event.

  • Ropes, canvas, corners and surrounding areas must be checked before competition.
  • Only authorized personnel may enter the ring during a bout.
  • Any safety concern must be reported to the event officials.
08+
Match FormatRounds, duration and rest periods.

Official Rule

The number of rounds, round duration and rest period will be announced for each PFS bout.

  • The official timekeeper controls round timing.
  • Fighters receive the scheduled rest period between rounds.
  • Championship and non-championship formats may have different schedules.
09+
ScoringOfficial judging and scorecards.

Official Rule

When a bout goes to the judges, it is evaluated using the scoring system announced for the PFS event.

  • Judges score the bout independently.
  • Official scorecards are submitted to the designated officials.
  • The announced result is based on the official scorecards and applicable regulations.
10+
OfficialsReferee, judges, timekeeper and announcer.

Official Rule

PFS events use designated officials to supervise the bout and record its official result.

  • The referee controls the action inside the ring.
  • Judges independently score the bout when required.
  • The timekeeper manages official round timing.
  • The announcer communicates official event information.
11+
Referee AuthorityControl, safety and enforcement inside the ring.

Official Rule

The referee has authority to manage the bout, enforce the rules and protect the fighters.

  • Fighters must follow referee instructions.
  • The referee may pause or stop action when required for safety or rule enforcement.
  • Failure to follow instructions may result in penalties under the event regulations.
12+
Legal Scoring AreasPermitted target areas for legal punches.

Official Rule

Only punches delivered to legal scoring areas under the applicable boxing rules count as legal offensive action.

  • Officials determine whether an action is legal.
  • Illegal target areas are not scoring areas.
  • Repeated illegal actions may result in warnings or penalties.
13+
FoulsIllegal actions and prohibited conduct.

Official Rule

Fighters must comply with the applicable boxing rules governing fouls and prohibited conduct.

  • Illegal strikes or conduct may be penalized.
  • Warnings, point deductions or disqualification may apply depending on the violation.
  • The referee determines the immediate in-ring enforcement.
14+
Warnings & PenaltiesHow rule violations are handled.

Official Rule

Officials may issue warnings or penalties when a fighter violates the rules.

  • A warning may be given for a minor or first violation.
  • Serious or repeated violations may lead to a point deduction.
  • Severe violations may affect the official result.
15+
Knockdown ProcedureOfficial response following a knockdown.

Official Rule

When a knockdown is recognized, the referee and timekeeper follow the applicable competition procedure.

  • The referee controls the count and action.
  • The standing fighter follows the referee's instructions.
  • The bout may continue or be stopped depending on the fighter's condition and applicable rules.
16+
Knockout (KO)Official result when a bout ends by knockout.

Official Rule

A knockout is recorded when the bout is ended under the applicable knockout criteria and the referee or designated official confirms the stoppage.

  • The referee's decision controls the in-ring stoppage.
  • The official result is recorded by the event officials.
  • Post-bout medical procedures must be followed.
17+
Technical Knockout (TKO)Safety-based or official stoppage.

Official Rule

A technical knockout may be recorded when an authorized official stops the bout because continuing is not appropriate under the rules.

  • The referee may stop the bout for safety.
  • A medical professional may recommend a stoppage where permitted.
  • The official result is recorded as required by the event regulations.
18+
Corner TeamApproved corner personnel and conduct.

Official Rule

Each fighter may have only the number of approved corner personnel permitted by the PFS event.

  • Corner personnel must remain in the designated area.
  • They must follow referee and event-official instructions.
  • Only authorized personnel may provide corner assistance.
19+
Corner ConductProfessional behavior during the bout.

Official Rule

Corner teams must conduct themselves professionally and must not interfere with the bout or officials.

  • No unauthorized person may enter the ring during active competition.
  • Instructions must remain within the permitted corner period.
  • Interference may result in penalties or removal from the event.
20+
StoppagesWhen and how a bout may be stopped.

Official Rule

A bout may be stopped by an authorized official when required by the rules, fighter safety or the circumstances of the competition.

  • The referee may stop action when necessary.
  • Medical personnel may be involved in safety-related decisions as permitted.
  • A stoppage is recorded according to the official result procedure.
21+
Result TypesOfficial ways a PFS bout can be decided.

Official Rule

Depending on the circumstances, a bout may be recorded by decision or by an official stoppage result.

  • Decision results are based on official judging.
  • Stoppage results are recorded by the applicable official.
  • The official result is announced after the bout.
22+
Draws & No ContestHandling exceptional official results.

Official Rule

Where the applicable rules require it, a bout may end in a draw or no contest rather than a winner being declared.

  • A draw is determined according to the official scoring procedure.
  • A no contest may apply where the rules require that outcome.
  • The final result is recorded by PFS event officials.
23+
Post-Fight ProcedureOfficial result, medical and event procedures.

Official Rule

After the bout, fighters and officials must complete the required post-fight procedures.

  • The official result is confirmed and recorded.
  • Required medical follow-up must be completed.
  • Fighters and corner teams must follow event-official instructions after the bout.
24+
Official AuthorityPFS event administration and final rulings.

Official Rule

PFS event officials administer these regulations and may issue event-specific instructions where necessary for safety, fairness and proper competition.

  • Event officials may clarify procedures before a bout.
  • Safety-related decisions must be respected.
  • Any future PFS rulebook revision may supersede this introductory version.
